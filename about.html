About Ouronova

We build like the people using it are counting on us — because they are.

Ouronova started as a small team of engineers frustrated by software that looked good in a pitch deck and fell apart in production. We set out to build differently: senior-led, outcome-accountable, and honest about tradeoffs.

Our Story

From side projects to a studio teams trust with their hardest problems.

Ouronova was founded on a simple premise: the gap between a good idea and a working product is almost always execution, not vision. We built the studio to close that gap for teams who don't have time to learn it the hard way.

Today we work with founders shipping their first product and enterprise teams modernizing systems that can't afford downtime — applying the same rigor to both.

"We measure ourselves by what still works eighteen months after we hand it over — not by what demos well on launch day."

— Ouronova Founding Principle
What we value

The principles behind every engagement.

Radical clarity

Every project starts with a scope, a timeline, and a definition of done — all in writing, all revisited weekly.

Craft over speed

We move fast because we don't cut corners twice. Clean architecture is faster in the long run, every time.

Ownership mentality

We treat your roadmap like our own product — flagging risks early instead of burying them in a status report.
