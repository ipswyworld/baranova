# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and enterprise teams (initially Kenya/East Africa-rooted, working with clients worldwide) who need a software, data, and strategy studio they can trust with production-critical work. They're evaluating whether this studio is senior, credible, and careful enough to hand real budget and real risk to — not browsing for inspiration.

## Product Purpose

Baranova (formerly Ouronova, renamed following a trademark conflict) is a Nairobi-based studio offering product engineering, data & analytics, and strategy/advisory services. The site's job is to convert that evaluation into a conversation: it must read as senior-led and outcome-accountable, not templated or generic. Success looks like a visitor coming away thinking "these people are careful enough to build something I can't afford to get wrong."

## Brand Personality

Warmer and more optimistic than the previous identity, without losing studio-grade credibility. Three words: **grounded, dawning, precise.**

- **Grounded**: "Bara" (Swahili: land/continent) roots the name in place, not abstraction. The brand should feel of-somewhere, not placeless-SaaS.
- **Dawning**: "Nova" + the tagline "A New Dawn" carry renewal, first light, a fresh start after the forced rename. This is real narrative material, not just a logo tagline; it can inform hero copy, motion (sunrise-coded reveals), and the origin story on About.
- **Precise**: the warmth must not soften the craft. This is still a studio pitching senior engineering and strategy work; polish, rigor, and restraint stay non-negotiable even as the palette and voice warm up.

The new mark's own device — a literal stitched **seam** between "BARA" and "NOVA" — is a deliberate brand element, not decoration. It should be reused intentionally across the product (section dividers, transitions, the splash screen) as a recurring motif that means something: two things joined, a continuation, a mended break.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-cream minimalism (the exact thing this same site was pulled back from earlier in its life as Ouronova).
- Touristy/stock "Africa" visual clichés: sunset acacia trees, zebra print, tribal-pattern wallpaper. The African-rootedness lives in color theory (red/gold/terracotta as historically high-value colors), the seam/textile-joinery metaphor, and language — not literal safari iconography.
- Cheesy or naive "new dawn" execution: no literal sunrise photography, no cursive script, nothing that reads as greeting-card. The dawn concept should show up as considered motion and color, not a cliché graphic.
- The site's own former restrained-only tone as a ceiling. Warmth is now permitted and expected; don't default back to the older, cooler register out of habit.

## Design Principles

1. **The seam is the system.** The stitch motif from the wordmark is a reusable brand device, not a one-off logo flourish — use it deliberately (dividers, transitions, the splash) wherever "joining two things" is the right idea.
2. **Warm, not soft.** Optimism and craft coexist. Every warmer/friendlier choice (Nunito, terracotta, rounder motion) still has to survive scrutiny from a CTO deciding whether to trust this studio with a rebuild.
3. **Complete break, not a coat of paint.** This is a legal rename. No lingering "Ouronova" anywhere — copy, metadata, repo name, URLs, alt text, JSON-LD, sitemap. A half-renamed site undercuts the reason for renaming at all.
4. **Dawn is a motion language, not a slogan.** "A New Dawn" earns its place by shaping how things reveal and transition (light, seam-join, emergence) — not by being printed as a tagline and otherwise ignored.
5. **Full craft pass, not spot fixes.** This rebrand is the occasion to raise the whole site's execution quality (typography, layout rhythm, motion, copy) — not just find-and-replace the name and palette.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the working bar (already the practice on this site): body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback, keyboard/focus-visible states on all interactive elements. The new terracotta (#C57B3C) must be re-verified for contrast in every context it's used (it's lighter than the gold it replaces) before shipping — do not assume it inherits the old gold's contrast safety.
